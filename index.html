Daily dares and badges

A new dare every day, streaks, badges, and a log of which sounds got the biggest laughs.

Friendly and private

No ads, no account, no internet permission. Everything works on your phone. Tooted is free; the optional one-time Tooted Plus adds unlimited own sounds and the Playlist trap.
