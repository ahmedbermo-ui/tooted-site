Tooted — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Tooted: Fart Prank Sounds is a prank soundboard for Android. It runs entirely on your phone. Tooted does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not have permission to use the internet.

Data we collect

None. Sounds you import or record, your playlists, your settings, badges, streak and laugh log stay on your phone, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Microphone — only while you press Record to make your own sound. The recording is saved on your phone and never sent anywhere.
  • Notifications — while a prank trap is loaded, Android shows a notification with a Cancel button. If you decline, the trap still works.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock — to keep a loaded trap ready with the screen off and play the sound when it triggers.
  • Vibration — the buzz when you tap a sound.

Motion, light and proximity sensors are read only while the Chair Trap or Sneaky Pull is loaded, on the phone, to know when to fire. Nothing is stored or sent. Tooted does not request location, contacts or photo access.

Purchases

Tooted Plus is an optional one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Tooted never sees your payment details. Google's handling of purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing and reviews

"Tell a friend" opens Android's share sheet with a link to Tooted on Google Play; you choose where it goes. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google Play.

Children

Tooted is meant for ages 13 and up. It does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version ever adds ads, this policy will be updated first.
